- patch-social-block (SOCIAL_TODO A7: Block and Mute): a person can now BLOCK and MUTE another person from the Social profile (`/social/u/<username>`: buttons Mute / Block, Block asks first; `SocialBlockButtons.tsx`, texts built in `socialBlockProps.ts`, server actions `setSocialBlock` / `setSocialMute` in `social/block/actions.ts`, rate limit `socialLimits.blocks` 30 per minute and 300 per day, Block and Mute counted together, server memory). MEANING (ACCOUNT_RULES.md 3.16, provisional): BLOCK = the two cannot interact: the blocked person cannot follow, like, repost, quote, reply to or mention the blocker (the refusal is the same "not found" as for a hidden post, it never says "blocked"), any follow between the two is removed in BOTH directions (Unblock does not bring it back), and the two never see each other's posts, reposts, replies or quotes (feeds, hashtag pages, post page, profile tabs, notifications). The blocked person gets "not found" on the blocker's profile and posts and is never told. The blocker sees a short page on that profile with "Blocked" (= Unblock). MUTE = the muter no longer sees the muted person's posts, reposts and replies in feeds, hashtag pages and under posts, and gets no notification (list and bell) caused by that person; nothing is refused to the muted person, nobody is told, the follow stays, the muted person's own profile and post pages still open (with a note on the profile). Unblock and unmute are always allowed. Rules in `social-block-rules.ts` (pure: `makeHiddenAuthors`, `dropHiddenItems`, `dropHiddenAuthors`), writes in `social-block.ts` (`applySocialBlock`, `applySocialMute`, `isBlockedEitherWay`, `eraseSocialBlocks`), reads in `social-block-view.ts` (`loadHiddenAuthors`, `getBlockRelation`). The shared feed caches hold no personal data, so the lists are applied AFTER the cache, per request: Home, `/social`, `/social/following`, `/social/tag/<tag>`, `/social/<id>` (replies, quotes; a blocked person's post is "not found"), the profile tabs (Posts and Replies hide only BLOCKED people, so a muted person's own profile still shows their posts; Likes hides blocked and muted; Followers / Following hide blocked people) and `/notifications` + the bell (`social-notifications.ts`). A page of a feed that loses all its items still shows "Older posts" when there are older ones. If the database fails the page fails; it never shows what the person blocked. The write paths ask ONE question, `isBlockedEitherWay`: `applySocialFollow` (follow only; unfollow is always allowed), like (`social/like/actions.ts`, like only; unlike is always allowed), `applySocialRepost` (repost and quote; undo is always allowed), `applySocialReply`, and `syncSocialTags` (a person who blocked the author, or was blocked by them, stays plain text in a mention and is not notified). Final erasure removes every block and mute made or received (`eraseSocialBlocks`, called from `purge-social.ts`). "Download my data" now lists the people the person blocked and muted (never who blocked them). DATABASE CHANGE: 2 new tables `SocialBlock` and `SocialMute` in the ready-made migration `20261111090000_social_block`: run `npx prisma migrate dev` (NOT with `--name`) and `npx prisma generate` inside web/. 16 new texts in six languages. New test `npm run test:social-block` (no database); the stand-in databases of `test-social-follow`, `test-social-repost`, `test-social-reply` and `test-social-tags` got an empty block table. Not done on purpose: a page listing everyone you blocked or muted (Settings), blocking or muting from a post card or from the Followers list, hiding the Community side, a limit on how many people one account may block, mute for a set time, muting words, "Block" for Community posts, hashtags and mentions in replies, a way for admins to see block counts (a person blocked by many may be a sign for moderators, decide later), the reply count and like count on a post still include blocked and muted people.
